The Advantages of the Diagnostic System

A technology that transforms thermoforming machine maintenance, reducing downtime and simplifying interventions

Key Advantages

Concrete benefits for your production

01

Drastic Reduction in Downtime

With the Optiform diagnostic system, the fault-finding phase is completely eliminated: the display clearly indicates which component is faulty.

Operational advantage: The technician knows exactly what to replace without opening the thermoforming machine, reducing machine downtime to the sole physical replacement of the component.
02

Maintenance Accessible to Everyone

No advanced electronics expertise or specialist technicians are needed to identify faults. The system shows the component code: anyone can read the information and proceed with the replacement by following the instructions.

Organisational advantage: Internal staff can handle most interventions without emergency calls to external technicians. Reduced support costs and greater operational autonomy.
03

Zero Diagnostic Errors

Manual diagnosis can lead to errors: healthy components replaced as a precaution, actual faults not identified, time wasted on unnecessary checks. The diagnostic system completely eliminates uncertainty.

Economic advantage: Only the actually faulty component is replaced. No wasted spare parts, no repeated interventions because the diagnosis was wrong.
04

Guaranteed Production Continuity

The system constantly monitors all heating elements in the background while the machine produces. It does not interfere with the operating cycle and allows anomalies to be identified as they arise.

Production advantage: No slowdowns for diagnostic checks. Production continues at full capacity while the system verifies the health status of the components. Maximum operational efficiency.
