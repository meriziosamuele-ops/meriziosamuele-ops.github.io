The Advantages of the Diagnostic System

A technology that transforms thermoforming machine maintenance, reducing downtime and simplifying interventions

Key Advantages

Concrete benefits for your production

01

Drastic Reduction of Downtime

The most costly problem in production is machine downtime. With the Optiform diagnostic system, the fault-finding phase is completely eliminated: the display immediately indicates which component is faulty.

Operational advantage: From hours of diagnosis to minutes of targeted intervention. The technician knows exactly what to replace before even opening the machine, reducing downtime to just the physical component replacement operation.
02

Maintenance Accessible to Everyone

Advanced electronic skills or specialized technicians are no longer needed to identify faults. The system displays the component code: anyone can read the information and proceed with replacement following the instructions.

Organizational advantage: Internal staff can handle most interventions without emergency calls to external technicians. Reduced assistance costs and greater operational autonomy.
03

Zero Diagnostic Errors

Manual diagnosis can lead to errors: healthy components replaced as a precaution, actual faults not identified, time wasted on useless checks. The diagnostic system completely eliminates uncertainty.

Economic advantage: Only the actually faulty component is replaced. No waste of spare parts, no repeated intervention because the diagnosis was wrong.
04

Guaranteed Production Continuity

The system constantly monitors all heating elements in the background while the machine produces. It doesn't interfere with the operational cycle and allows immediate identification of anomalies when they occur.

Production advantage: No slowdowns for diagnostic checks. Production continues at full capacity while the system verifies the health status of components. Maximum operational efficiency.
