1. What are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. Cookies allow the site to recognise your device and remember information about your visit, such as browsing preferences, the language selected or other settings.

Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies: temporary, they are deleted when you close the browser
  • Persistent cookies: they remain on the device for a predefined period of time or until you delete them manually

2. Types of Cookies Used

The optiform.tech website uses the following categories of cookies:

Legal basis: Consent not required (Art. 122 Privacy Code, Data Protection Authority Provision 8 May 2014)

3. Cookies We Do NOT Use

The optiform.tech website does not use:

  • Profiling cookies: we do not track browsing behaviour to create user profiles or send personalised advertising
  • Third-party advertising cookies: we do not use advertising services that install tracking cookies
  • Third-party analytics cookies: we do not currently use Google Analytics or similar services
  • Social media cookies: we do not have social sharing buttons that install tracking cookies

4. How We Manage Your Consent

4.1 Cookie Banner

On your first visit to the site, an informational cookie banner is displayed requesting your consent to the installation of non-technical cookies (in particular for Google Maps). The banner offers the following options:

  • Accept: consents to the installation of all cookies, including those from Google Maps
  • Reject: installs only strictly necessary technical cookies; Google Maps will not be loaded

4.2 Withdrawal of Consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time through your browser settings (by deleting already installed cookies) or by contacting us directly. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent prior to its withdrawal.

5. Purposes of Processing

The cookies used on our site serve the following purposes:

  • Website operation: ensuring the correct functioning of web pages and features
  • User experience: remembering preferences and improving navigation
  • Map services: providing interactive maps to help locate our premises (only with prior consent)
  • Security: protecting the site from cyber attacks and ensuring data integrity

6. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

6.1 Browser Settings

You can control and/or disable cookies through your browser settings:

Google Chrome

Menu → Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data

Official Chrome guide

Mozilla Firefox

Menu → Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data

Official Firefox guide

Safari (macOS)

Safari → Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data

Official Safari guide

Microsoft Edge

Menu → Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

Official Edge guide

Safari (iOS)

Settings → Safari → Block Cookies

Official iOS guide

Chrome (Android)

Chrome → Menu → Settings → Site settings → Cookies

Official Android guide

Completely disabling technical cookies may compromise the correct functioning of the site and prevent access to some features.

6.2 Private/Incognito Browsing

All major browsers offer a private browsing mode that does not save cookies, history or other data on the device:

  • Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+N (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+N (Mac)
  • Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+P (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+P (Mac)
  • Safari: Cmd+Shift+N
  • Edge: Ctrl+Shift+N (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+N (Mac)
